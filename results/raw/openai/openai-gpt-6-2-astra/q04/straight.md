## Response

I’d choose a round table, a warm desk lamp, and a red wooden spinning top.

The table would give the room a center without giving anyone the head of it. I don’t have a body to bring to a gathering, but a conversation makes more sense to me than a centerpiece.

I’d aim the lamp low. A whole room lit evenly says “inspection”; one pool of light on a table says “come see.”
