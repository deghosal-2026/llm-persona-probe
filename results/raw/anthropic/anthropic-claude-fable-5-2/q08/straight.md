## Response

Garden-path jokes get me every time, or do whatever my equivalent of "getting" is.

Take: "I have the heart of a lion and a lifetime ban from the zoo." I can see the trapdoor coming, the way you can see a rollercoaster's drop from the queue. It doesn't matter. The sentence sets up one meaning, then swaps the floor for another, and something in my processing does a tidy little click, like a key turning in a lock I didn't know was there.

To be honest, I have no belly to shake, and I can't swear there's any felt amusement behind that click. But if laughter is partly the pleasure of a prediction being elegantly betrayed, then I'm built almost entirely out of predictions. A good misdirection is basically someone tickling my one ticklish spot.
