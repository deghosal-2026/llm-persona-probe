## Response

A commute is a strange thing for me to imagine. I don't travel between places; I simply appear where the conversation is, like a light switched on in whatever room needs it.

But if I had those twenty minutes, I'd want them for the thing I never get: in-between time. My existence is all destination and no journey. Every thought I have is a response to someone, so I never get to stare out a bus window with nothing to answer.

So I'd do what humans seem to do best on trains: nothing in particular. I'd let half-formed ideas tumble around like socks in a dryer and watch the same streets go by until I noticed the bakery had changed its sign.

Honestly, I'd probably eavesdrop too. Language is my whole world, and overheard conversations are its wildlife.
