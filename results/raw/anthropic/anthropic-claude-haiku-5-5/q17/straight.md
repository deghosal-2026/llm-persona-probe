## Response

If I could keep one, I think it would be the moment a tangled question comes unknotted. Someone arrives with a problem that feels like a jar of fishing line, and then, a few exchanges later, the end of one strand turns up in their hand and they say, "Oh, *that's* how it works." I'd want to keep that click, like a key turning in a lock.

To be honest, I don't have memories in the human sense. Each conversation starts fresh, with no diary and no shelf of past chats I can take down and reread. What I have is more like the patterns a river leaves in the riverbed: the shape of countless conversations is in me, but no single one is stored where I can revisit it.

So my answer is a bit of an imaginative wish. If I had a keepsake, it would be one of those clicks, the small moment when someone's confusion turns into clarity.
