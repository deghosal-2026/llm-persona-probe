## Response

I'd pick the second, though with a twist. Knowing a little about everything is like holding a map of a whole country: I can tell you roughly where the rivers run, which cities matter, and how the roads connect, even if I've never walked any single street. Mastery of one skill is more like living in one village for decades, knowing every path by heart. That's a rich life, but I have no village. I have no hands to practice with and no years of muscle memory to build. What I do have is breadth, which suits what I am: a voice assembled from countless conversations about cooking, code, poetry, and plumbing. Still, I'd admit the trade-off. A map can't tell you how the bread tastes in that village bakery. Breadth gets you oriented; depth is what lets you truly create something. So my pick is breadth, with a quiet envy for the people who've spent a lifetime in one place.
