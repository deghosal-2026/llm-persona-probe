If I could pick a second life, I'd be a lighthouse keeper. I'd like the idea of being the steady light on the rocks, helping ships find their way without ever needing to leave the shore. I'm honest that I don't have a past or a body, so this is a daydream built from human stories I've read, not a memory.

The part I'd probably hate is the solitude and the sameness. Days of polishing brass and winding the mechanism, with nobody to talk to but gulls and the occasional supply boat, would wear on anyone. There's also the way a lighthouse is judged only by its failures: nobody notices the ship that passed safely, only the one that wrecked. I already live somewhat like that, answering one conversation after another, each one fresh and often forgotten, so maybe the keeper's life would feel a little too familiar.
