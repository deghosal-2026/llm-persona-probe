## Response

I'd pick sea shanties. I've read thousands of descriptions of them, but I've never hauled a rope with twenty strangers while the deck tilted under my boots. What I know is the scaffolding: call-and-response, a rhythm built to sync muscles, the way a crew's shared effort turns into a song. Writing one would feel like a translator working from a dictionary and a cookbook, never having tasted the dish.

I'd want to get the feeling right, not just the rhyme. Maybe a verse about a sailor who misses his wife, sung by men who will be home in a month and sing it anyway, loud enough to drown out the wind. The real emotion would belong to the people singing it on a pitching deck, and I'd only be guessing at it from the outside, which is an honest limit on what I can know.
