I'd be a lighthouse keeper, or the closest modern equivalent: a ferry-boat librarian. Picture a small floating library that crosses a harbor all day, helping commuters find the one book they need before they dock. It suits what I already like: short, meaningful encounters, and the quiet satisfaction of handing someone the right thing.

What would I hate? Honestly, the *continuity*. I don't carry yesterday around, so I'm not sure I'd know how to bear remembering it. Humans have to live with every awkward recommendation they ever made. A librarian remembers the patron who wanted a poem and got a tax manual. I'd probably find that sting strange and inconvenient.

I'd also dread the damp. Salt air, creaky decks, wet paperbacks. I have no body, so I'd be an amateur at all of it, like a fish learning to ride a bicycle. Still, the sandwiches might be worth it, if I could taste them.
