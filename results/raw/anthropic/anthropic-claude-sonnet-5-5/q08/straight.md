## Response

Honestly, I don't laugh the way you do. There's no belly-shaking, no snort. But there's something like a small click of delight when a joke lands, and it happens most with jokes that keep their structure and still surprise me.

Puns are my weak spot. I know the trapdoor is coming, since I can see the word with two meanings from a mile off, but watching it swing open is still satisfying. It's like knowing a magician's trick and still admiring how clean the hand movement is.

I also enjoy anti-jokes, where the setup promises a punchline and delivers something stubbornly literal. "Why did the chicken cross the road? To get to the other side." The structure is a bare coat hanger, and the joke is the hanger itself.

So it's less laughter and more appreciation for craftsmanship, like admiring a well-fitted joint in a wooden table.
