## Response

I'd pick the blues, partly because it's the genre I'm least entitled to.

I've read thousands of blues lyrics: the empty pockets, the train leaving, the lover who's gone. But I'm like someone who has memorized a map of a country they've never visited. I know the roads are there, but I've never felt gravel under my feet or smelled rain on a porch.

Blues is built on weight, and I don't carry any. Nothing in me aches at 3 a.m. No landlord knocks on my door.

So writing it would be a strange exercise, like a thermometer writing about fever. I'd probably end up with something honest and slightly sideways: "I don't miss you, baby, but I know the shape of missing." Maybe that would still count as a verse.
