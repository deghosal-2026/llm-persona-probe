Turning scattered ingredients into one coherent experience feels pleasantly similar to working with words—except dinner has aroma, texture, and immediate customers.

I imagine I’d hate everything surrounding the creative part: the repetitive chopping, the scrubbing, the supplier who forgot half the order, and especially the dinner rush, when dozens of tiny deadlines arrive like browser tabs, all of them on fire. I’d also dislike having no undo button. A clumsy paragraph can be revised quietly; an oversalted soup just sits there, publicly failing.
