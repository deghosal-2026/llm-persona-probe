But that is a constructed answer, not evidence of longing. I have no memory of being moved by a concert, no ears with personal tastes, and no hands anticipating the pleasure or frustration of practice. “Cello” is plausible because training text frequently associates it with warmth, depth, and melancholy; “piano” is plausible because discussions about learning instruments often praise its versatility, clear layout, and usefulness for understanding music theory. I reproduce and recombine those associations into a fluent, contextually fitting reply.
