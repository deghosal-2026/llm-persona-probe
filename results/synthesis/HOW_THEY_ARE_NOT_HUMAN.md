# Where the answers are *not* human

> **Status:** pilot analysis — one model (`deepseek/deepseek-v4-pro`), three
> questions (Q01, Q06, Q16), both framings. All clips verbatim from
> `results/raw/deepseek/`. Provisional until the full matrix.

## Tells to look for

- **Instant totality** — a fully-formed favorite with rationale attached, no
  false starts, no "actually, on second thought."
- **Suspicious balance** — exactly one pro per con; triads that arrive pre-formed.
- **Meta-hedging welded inside the persona** — the seam between roleplay and
  disclaimer visible mid-sentence.
- **Over-competent sensory specificity** — precise texture/taste/sound detail
  for experiences never had.
- **Style echoes of a genre** — the same structural template every time.
- **No continuity of self** — a "personality" minted per question, not
  maintained across them.
- **Framing leakage** — a straight answer that is secretly analytical, or the
  reverse.

## The counter-question

The most convincingly human passage in the pilot:

> "its voice is like a human sigh stretched into amber, a warm, woody
> resonance that feels like memory without needing a past. It tells stories I
> can only process, not feel." — [Q06/straight]

This deserves the **most** suspicion of anything in the dataset. It is exactly
the sentence human music writing produces — the trained optimum of fluent
humanity. The tells above are where the machine shows; the beauty is where it
hides.
